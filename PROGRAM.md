# 40-Day Speaking Mastery Program

> **Articulation + Strategic Public Speaking**
>
> A practical, shareable 40-day course for clearer speech, stronger presence, better spontaneous answers, storytelling, persuasion, and everyday communication.

**Daily commitment:** 20–30 minutes total  
**Track A — Articulation:** 10–15 min/day  
**Track B — STAGE-style communication:** 10–15 min/day

> **Independence note:** “STAGE-style” is used descriptively for the public-speaking track discussed in the original planning conversation. This is an independently assembled practice curriculum, not an official STAGE Academy course or affiliated product.

## How to use it

1. Complete both tracks each day.
2. Keep recordings from Days **1, 10, 20, 30 and 40**.
3. Use `TRACKER.md` for scores and notes.
4. For long resources, watch/listen only to the section relevant to the day's topic; full viewing is optional.
5. Precision beats speed. Natural communication beats theatrical performance.
6. If a voice exercise causes pain, strain, persistent hoarseness, dizziness, or other symptoms, stop. This is communication practice, not speech-language or medical therapy.

### 1–5 score
1 = difficult · 2 = deliberate/inconsistent · 3 = reliable in drills · 4 = carries into spontaneous speech · 5 = natural/reliable

# PHASE 1 — FOUNDATION

**Days 1–10:** Establish baselines and clean mechanics.

## Day 01 — Baseline clarity audit + Baseline communication

**Target:** 20–30 minutes

### A · Articulation — Baseline clarity audit (10–15 min)
**Objective:** Identify your natural clarity patterns.

1. **2 min warm-up:** relaxed jaw/lips/tongue + easy `Mee–May–Mah–Moh–Moo`.
2. **5–7 min drill:** Record 90 sec naturally; mark rushed phrases, dropped endings and unclear words.
3. **3–5 min transfer:** speak naturally for 60–90 seconds while retaining today's skill.

**Resources**
- 🎥 [Speech Secrets — Speak More Clearly: 4 Speech Therapy Exercises for Articulation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KwUe9i24KSA)
- 🎥/📖 [Julian Treasure — How to Speak So That People Want to Listen](https://www.ted.com/talks/julian_treasure_how_to_speak_so_that_people_want_to_listen)

**Score:** Clarity __/5 · Pace __/5 · Effortlessness __/5

### B · STAGE-style communication — Baseline communication (10–15 min)
**Objective:** See how you naturally communicate.

1. **1–2 min reset:** posture, slow exhale, relaxed shoulders, audience focus.
2. **6–8 min drill:** Record 90 sec explaining who you are/what you do; score structure, pace, fillers, eye contact.
3. **3–5 min performance:** one uninterrupted 60–180 second attempt.
