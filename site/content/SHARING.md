# Sharing & Group Use

This package is designed to work well with family, friends, coworkers, or a small study group.

## Suggested group rules
- Everyone completes the same numbered day.
- Recordings are shared only when the participant wants to share them.
- Feedback = **one observed strength + one specific next improvement**.
- Comment on observable communication behavior: clarity, pace, pauses, structure, eye contact, fillers, voice, storytelling, or concision.
- Do not critique someone's accent as an identity marker; focus on intelligibility and effectiveness.

## 20-minute meetup
1. **3 min** — warm-up together
2. **5 min** — articulation drill
3. **8 min** — two or three speaking attempts
4. **4 min** — feedback + next-day commitment

## Partner feedback prompts
- What was the clearest part?
- Where did I lose the thread?
- Did I answer the question quickly enough?
- Which words/ideas landed?
- Where would a pause help?
- What should I keep exactly as-is?
